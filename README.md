Vice Heat Cover Studio: an offline web app that turns any photo and caption into GTA 6-style multi-panel cover art — 10 layouts (including Key Art Cast, the default: one dominant hero, six supporting panels, title printed over the art), 7 Vice grades with strength/contrast/saturation/grain/vignette sliders (Sunset Punch locked as the house grade so every panel shares one golden-hour color story), 7 library photos each with its own cinematic scene beat (place + time + one loaded line, e.g. "PIER 26 · 5:30 AM · THE CATCH WAS NEVER FISH") and character label, a press-quote strip with star rating, a film-tagline logline, poster furniture (EST. seal, VOL. pill, barcode, credits), tracked 3D lettering with an orange-to-pink gradient face, film grain and vignette, undo/redo and a Surprise me button, with PNG export in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Everything runs offline from the files under app/; photos stay on your machine.
