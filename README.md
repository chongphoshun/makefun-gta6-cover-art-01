Vice Heat Cover Studio: an offline web app that turns any photo and caption into GTA 6-style multi-panel cover art — 9 layouts (including Whole Cast: hero + 6-panel grid), 7 Vice grades (including Comic Ink and Sunset Punch) with strength/contrast/saturation/grain/vignette sliders, scene captions and character labels on every panel, a press-quote strip, poster furniture (EST. seal, VOL. pill, barcode, credits), 3D lettering, film grain and vignette, undo/redo and a Surprise me button, with PNG export in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Everything runs offline from the files under app/; photos stay on your machine.
